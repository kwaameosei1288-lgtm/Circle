Helping Hands Circle Constitution

Our guiding principles and rules

1. Name and Purpose

The organization shall be known as "Helping Hands Circle – Stronger Together". Our purpose is to provide mutual support, financial assistance, and welfare services to our members.

2. Membership

Membership is open to all individuals who share our values and commit to our principles. Members must accept this constitution and pay monthly contributions.

3. Contributions

Each member shall contribute GHS 50.00 monthly. Contributions are due by the first week of each month. Late payments incur a GHS 10.00 penalty.

4. Welfare Support

The organization provides welfare support for marriage (GHS 2000), funeral of parents (GHS 3000), serious sickness (GHS 1000), and other approved cases.

5. Leadership

The organization shall have the following roles: Admin, Treasurer, Secretary, Welfare Officer, and Assistant Welfare Officer.

6. Decision Making

Major decisions require consensus among leadership. Welfare requests are approved by welfare officers.

7. Code of Conduct

Members must conduct themselves with integrity, respect, and commitment to helping others. Any breach may result in suspension or expulsion.

8. Amendments

This constitution may be amended by majority vote of active members.

Constitution Acceptance
